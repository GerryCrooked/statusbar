Bar on the desktops

      

        

          

        

      

      

        

        

        

      

      
      
      
      
      
Applies to the classic Remote Desktop client. "Hide" (default) needs the client installed on your session hosts as well – without it, users in a full-screen session see no message; choose "Show the bar on top of it" then. Sessions across several monitors hide the bar on each of them. "Take out of full screen" only acts on single-monitor sessions and restores full screen after the all-clear.

      
      

        
        
        
        
        
      

      
      

    

    

      
Silent alarm

      
A user presses a key combination on their computer and the chosen recipients get a message at once. Nothing appears on the computer where it was pressed. For reception desks, counters and anyone who may need help without drawing attention.

      
      
      
      
Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Win plus a function key (Ctrl+Shift+F12), or two of them plus a character (Ctrl+Shift+Ü – only on keyboard layouts that have it). Choose one nobody presses by accident and that no other program uses.

      
      
      
      
      
{user} and {computer} are replaced by who pressed the keys and where.

      

        

        

      

      
      

      
While an alarm from a computer is open, pressing again there changes nothing. At most 5 alarms per 10 minutes are raised on this server; further ones are only logged.

      
      

    

    

      
Sounds

      
Desktops play a sound when a new message appears – once, for the most urgent new one. Windows sounds follow each computer's sound scheme and volume. Your own sounds: WAV, up to 1 MB. Never on the computer that raised a silent alarm.

Ticket system

      
Statusbar opens a ticket when a message is published or a silent alarm is raised, and adds a note and closes it when the message is resolved. Tickets opened here never come back as a second message through an inbound ticket integration.

An answer with "id" and "url" is shown with the message.
